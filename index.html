The Roots — Gafo Abio (Father of Guracha)
Before Guracha came his father Gafo Abio, who had two wives. His first wife Haduyo bore five children including Abio Gafo, later known as Guracha. His second wife Rumbe Kofa bore one child, Hadia Gafo.

Abio Gafo (Guracha) — His Four Wives & Their Children
Guracha married four wives at different stages of his life — not simultaneously. Each marriage began after the previous had ended. He passed away in 1971 and was laid to rest in Mombasa.
